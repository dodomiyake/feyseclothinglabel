# NERSI explainer: fact sheet

Every claim in the film, with the official wording it rests on. Source: the official NERSI page, https://fmhapr.gov.ng/nersi/ (Federal Ministry of Humanitarian Affairs and Poverty Reduction), read 6 Nov 2026.

| In the film | Official wording |
|---|---|
| ₦50,000 grant, not a loan | "How much will I receive? ₦50,000. The full amount goes into your wallet. Nothing is deducted." · "Is it a loan? No. It is a grant from the Federal Government. You do not pay it back." |
| For poor and vulnerable households | "₦50,000 support for poor and vulnerable households, sent straight to your phone. No fees. No middlemen." |
| Chosen from the National Social Register and verified identity records, paid in phases across six zones | "Beneficiaries are selected from the National Social Register and verified national identity records." · "Beneficiaries are spread across all six geopolitical zones … Payments go out in phases" |
| You cannot apply; no one can sell you a place | "You cannot apply or pay to be added." · "nobody can sell you a place on the list." |
| SMS from your provider, accept, money in your wallet | "You get an SMS. It comes from ALAT, MoMo, NairaGram, OPay or Moniepoint" · "Reply YES" · "₦50,000 is credited." The SMS quote is the example given in the FAQ. |
| Codes: ALAT *945*23#, MoMo *671*0#, NairaGram *726*00#, OPay *955*20#, Moniepoint *5573*27# | "Collecting your money" section, verbatim |
| Check eligibility by dialling a code | "You can also dial your provider's code and choose Check eligibility." |
| Works on any phone, no data | "No. A basic phone is enough. The codes work without the internet." |
| Free; never pay, never share PIN/OTP, never click a link | "This programme is free." · "It never asks for money, your PIN, your OTP or for you to click a link." |
| Help line 0700-022-2222 | Page header: "Need help? Call 0700-022-2222" |

Deliberately left out: whether the payment is one-off or monthly. The page text read does not state a frequency, and press reports disagree.
